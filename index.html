How to read the model

Three figures, three different questions

1

Level-service capacity

Uses the smallest annual opening across the term. It is the conservative structure and can understate a stepped repayment plan.

2

Annual wrapped capacity

Fits new payments into each year’s projected opening. It is a ceiling for structuring discussions, not a borrowing recommendation.

3

Referendum replacement

Shows debt service that may roll off but cannot be treated as ordinary capacity without the required voter and legal approvals.
